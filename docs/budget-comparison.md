# Monthly budget comparison

Budget-relevant spending = total recorded actual spending minus expenses explicitly marked Unexpected Occasional. Regular, Planned Occasional, Unknown and NA expense types are included. Transfers and duplicate transaction IDs are excluded; signed refunds stay in their type.

Amounts are compared in cents. Under/over/exact results and daily allowance use this same figure for current and past months. Unknown types no longer suppress outcomes or produce a range. A compact unclassified amount links to a month-specific popup ordered by largest amount, where existing transaction editing saves classification exceptions. Suggested budget calculation remains based on classified history and reports its coverage separately.

The month card shows total actual spending, the amount compared against budget, unexpected spending excluded, and a short unclassified review link. Detailed category breakdowns remain accessible by tapping the actual total.
