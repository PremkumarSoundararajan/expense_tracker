Delete your Pocket Ledger account

You can request deletion without the mobile app. Open Pocket Ledger on the web, sign in with the Google account you used for the app, and confirm deletion. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Open account deletion

Or in the app: Settings → Delete account and app data.

What is deleted

  • The app’s “Expense Tracker DB” spreadsheet, including expense history, categories, merchant classifications and spending targets.
  • Active server records and any previous email-scanning authorizations.
  • Saved app data, budgets and reminders on the device used for deletion.
  • Pocket Ledger’s Google authorization. You are signed out when deletion completes.

Your Google account, original statement files and unrelated spreadsheets are not deleted. Keep a separate copy of your records first if needed. Deletion is permanent.

Timing and other copies

Deletion normally completes while you wait. Progress and any failures are shown in the app. If interrupted, sign in again and resume deletion. Offline copies on other devices cannot be erased remotely; sign out on those devices and clear Pocket Ledger’s app/site data there. Do not continue using an older app with a cached ledger after deleting your account.

Cloudflare database recovery backups may retain deleted records for up to 30 days, depending on the service plan. Those backups expire under the provider’s retention policy. No receipt data or tokens are intentionally retained in active app storage after successful deletion. Google’s own retention policies apply to its systems.

Need help? Contact the developer about account deletion. Never send passwords or access tokens.
